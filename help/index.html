Help

Email support@dailahub.com and we'll get back to you.

Signing in

  • Forgot your password? Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link.
  • Signing in with your phone: enter your mobile number and we'll text you a one-time code. Codes expire after 5 minutes. Never share a code with anyone; Daila Hub will never ask you for it.
  • Didn't get a code? Check that the number is right, wait a minute, then request a new one. If you replied STOP before, reply START to turn text messages back on.

Text messages

  • We text you only when you ask for a sign-in code. We never send marketing texts.
  • Reply STOP to stop all texts. Reply START to turn them back on. Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply. See the SMS terms.

Guests and RSVPs

  • Need to change your reply? Open the event page and use "Email me my RSVP link". The link works for 15 minutes.
  • Want to be removed from an event? Ask the host, or email us.
  • Got an email you didn't expect? Someone may have added your address to their guest list. You can ignore it, or tell us.

Your account

  • Update your profile, sign out of every device, or delete your account in your account settings.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your billing settings.
  • To ask for a copy or the deletion of your information, see the Privacy Policy.
