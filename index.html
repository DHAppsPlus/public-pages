Daila Hub

Plan weddings, birthdays, galas and every gathering in between. Create an event page, invite your guests, collect RSVPs and check guests in at the door.

What Daila Hub does

  • Event pages you can design and share with your guests.
  • Invitations by email, with RSVP collection and guest lists.
  • Seating charts and a team you can invite to help run your event.
  • Door check-in with entry passes.

Signing in by text message

Daila Hub can sign you in with a one-time verification code sent by text message to your mobile number. We send codes only when you ask for one. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help. See our SMS terms and Privacy Policy.
